Reading the record correctly

Publication is not review and not approval. A submitted symbol is public immediately, so visibility never means verified. Recognition counts are post exposure recognition, not independent replication. Curated example rows are operator added illustrations and are excluded from every convergence total. The three status fields, visibility, moderation and evidence, are independent and must not be collapsed into one badge.
